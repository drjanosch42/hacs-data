# `integral_hecke_dataset/v1` — the integral Hecke structure data set

**Abstract.** For a congruence subgroup $\Gamma_0(N)$ and the local system $\mathrm{Sym}^n\mathbb Z^2$, the Hecke operators generate a
commutative ring $\mathbb T$ acting on the free part of $H^1(\Gamma_0(N);\mathrm{Sym}^n\mathbb Z^2)$. This data set records, cell by cell, what
is known with proof about $\mathbb T$ *over $\mathbb Z$*: its rational shape (the components of $\mathbb T\otimes\mathbb Q$ and their minimal
polynomials), its local shape at each prime $\ell$ where something can happen (the maximal ideals, their residue fields and local rings, the
places where components meet), the Eisenstein congruence module, and how the torsion of the cohomology sits relative to the free part. Every
statement carries a status that says whether it is proved, bounded, or waiting for a named piece of work. The tables are derived from records
of the schema `integral_hecke_structure/v1` ([C] `sec:recordschema` item 8) and reproduce them exactly; the records themselves travel with the
data set.

Builder: `docs/integral_census/mk_hecke_dataset.py` (`build`, `verify`, `selftest`). Independent check: `docs/integral_census/hz_dataset_verify.py`.
Editions: `results/hecke_ed1/` (edition 1). The companion data set of the cohomology groups themselves is `integral_census_dataset/v1`
(`dataset_v1.md`, `results/ed1/`).

## 1. What a cell and a record are

A **cell** is a pair $(N, n)$ with $n$ even ($n$ odd gives zero), weight $k = n + 2$. For each cell there are one or two **records**, one per
**algebra**:

* `anemic` — $\mathbb T^{\mathrm{an}}$, generated by the $T_p$ with $p\nmid N$;
* `full` — $\mathbb T$, generated in addition by the $U_p$ with $p\mid N$.

At level $N = 1$ the two coincide and only `full` is recorded. The key of a record is `N<N>_n<n>_<algebra>` (column `key` in every table).

The integers attached to a record: $b_1$ (the rank of the free part of $H^1$, from the integral descent), $e_k$ (the Eisenstein
contribution) and $d$ (the rank of the algebra over $\mathbb Z$; `d_source` says how it was obtained).

* For `full`, $d$ is the rank of $\mathbb T$, i.e. the dimension of $M_k(\Gamma_0(N))$ (`d_source = hES_N`), and $b_1 = 2d - e_k$.
* For `anemic`, $d$ is the rank of $\mathbb T^{\mathrm{an}}$ (`d_source = hES_N_an`). It is smaller than the `full` $d$ when old forms of different levels share their anemic eigensystem (the $U_p$ are
  what separates them), and then $b_1 = 2d - e_k$ **fails**: $b_1$ is a property of the cohomology, not of the algebra. In edition 1 the
  identity holds for all 141 `full` records and for 44 of the 85 `anemic` ones; it fails for the other 41, e.g. $(2,4)$ anemic with
  $b_1 = 2$, $d = 1$, $e_k = 2$, or $(3,12)$ anemic with $b_1 = 8$, $d = 4$, $e_k = 2$.

## 2. How a statement is qualified: hash layers and statuses

A record has three layers. The **hashed body** (field groups G0–G8; FNV-1a-64 `record_fnv1a64`) holds the mathematics; the **certificate
layer** (G9; `cert_sha256`) holds what an independent checker (the Lean lane) needs; the **provenance** (G10) records how the run went and is
in neither hash. The **settings fingerprint** names the mathematical settings and caps the record was computed under; one edition has one
fingerprint.

Every structural field carries a word of one closed list. Each word is **final** (no planned work moves it: `certified`, `upper_bound_certified`,
`bounds`, `zero`, `distinct_certified`, `common_certified`, `agrees_on_P`, `partial(unfactored)`, `open(…)`, `refused(by_theory)`,
`refused(word_modulus)`, `refused(no_lift)`, `refused(precision)`, `not_run`, …) or **pending** (a named later package moves it:
`blocked(U_p)` — needs $U_p$ over $\mathbb Z$; `blocked(export)`, `refused(export_word)`, `refused(k1_route)`, `refused(word_prime_cap)`).
A record is `pending` iff one of its hashed fields is pending, `refused` iff the record itself is refused, `final` otherwise. Only final
records are golden-eligible. The record never writes an exact value where only a bound is proved.

## 3. The tables, in the order of the mathematics

### 3.1 The rational structure — `rows.tsv`, `components.tsv`

`rows.tsv` has one line per record. Beside the key and the integers of §1:

* `C1`, `C1_deg_f` — (C1): a primitive element $\theta$ (`theta`, coefficients on the operator set `P`) whose characteristic polynomial has
  the full degree $d$, so $\mathbb T\otimes\mathbb Q=\mathbb Q[\theta]$; `reduced_Q` says whether $\mathbb T\otimes\mathbb Q$ is reduced.
* `C2_status` — (C2): whether the square part of the discriminant gcd of the primitive operators (`disc_bits`, `disc_fnv1a64`) is fully
  factored (`complete`) or leaves a cofactor (`partial(unfactored)`, `cofactor_test`).
* `S_cand` — the primes where $\mathbb T$ may fail to be étale or where congruences may live (discriminant square part, torsion primes,
  congruence primes). Only these primes are examined locally (§3.2).
* `components` — the number of minimal primes of $\mathbb T\otimes\mathbb Q$ (or the status word when they are not available, e.g.
  `blocked(U_p)`). `components.tsv` lists each: `type` (`eis` / `cusp`), `degree`, `mult` on $H^1$, the operator `p0` whose eigenvalue
  generates the coefficient field, the `minpoly` of that eigenvalue (constant term first, canonical signed hex) with its FNV digest, and
  `lambda_theta` for a rational component.
* The Eisenstein congruence summary (§3.3): `partition`, `S`, `S_prime`, `C_T_status`, `C_T_upper`, `C_T_exact`, `C_T_lower`,
  `fibre_product`, `general_case`.
* Status: `status`, `refusal`, `n_pending` and `pending_words` (each pending word with its count), `status_words` (all status words of the
  hashed body with counts), `golden_eligible`, `golden15` (one of the fifteen level-1 cells pinned for the first golden event).
* Handles: `record_fnv1a64`, `cert_sha256`, the descent reference (`descent_record_fnv1a64`, `descent_cell_sha256`, `descent_join` = `ed1`
  or `descent_bundle`, §3.8), `classes` (the cell classes C0–C9 of the run plan), `rat_primes_used`, `lane`, `seconds`, `rss_mib`, and `pass`, `source_pin`
  (which run pass and source commit wrote the record, §3.8).

### 3.2 The local structure at a prime $\ell$ — `badset.tsv`, `maxideals.tsv`, `pairs.tsv`

`badset.tsv`: one line per (record, $\ell$) for $\ell$ in `S_cand` or read in G2. `reasons` and `v_disc` (why $\ell$ is a candidate),
the exponent and primality proof of $\ell$ in the discriminant gcd; then the local read: `theory` (`H`, the free and torsion parts of
$H^1$), `generation` (whether the operators read generate $\mathbb T\otimes\mathbb Z_\ell$, and by which `route`: `saturation`,
`eis_single`, `rational`, …), `dim_A1` (the $\mathbb F_\ell$-dimension of the algebra read mod $\ell$; `dim_A1_source` = `A1_of_computed`
when generation is not certified — then it is a statement about the computed subalgebra only), `t_ell` (whether $T_\ell$ was in the
operator set), `maximal_ideals` (`certified` when the decomposition is proved, else `not_run`), `decomposed_by`, `n_ideals`, and the
provenance `note`.

`maxideals.tsv`: one line per maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ above $\ell$: the residue field ($\mathbb F_{\ell^f}$, `residue_degree` $f$,
`residue_poly` the monic factor mod $\ell$ of the generating operator's polynomial), `support` (`free`, `torsion` or `both`), the local ring
$\mathbb T_{\mathfrak m}$ — `kind` (`etale`, `monogenic`, `non_monogenic`), `rank` over $\mathbb Z_\ell$, `length` of
$\mathbb T_{\mathfrak m}/\ell$, `embdim`, `gorenstein`, `hilbert`, `loewy` — and `components_through` (which component types pass through
$\mathfrak m$, with their status).

`pairs.tsv`: one line per pair of components meeting at $\mathfrak m$: the `depth` $v_\ell\operatorname{Res}$ of their polynomials (the
congruence exponent), its `status`, and `kind` when known.

### 3.3 Congruence modules — `congruence.tsv`

The Eisenstein part and the rest ($\mathcal S$, $\mathcal S'$) meet in the congruence module $C_{\mathbb T}$. Record-level values are in
`rows.tsv` (`C_T_upper` = $g_P$ factored, `C_T_exact` when lower and upper bounds agree at every prime). `congruence.tsv` gives one line
per (record, scope): `scope = ell=<ℓ>` — the $\ell$-part: `lower_v`, `upper_v`, `read`, `status`, the Smith exponents of $\eta_L$
(`eta_exponents`, `eta_r`, `eta_sign`), and the independent second route (`v_Bk` = $v_\ell(B_k/k)$ at level 1, `agrees`; `mazur_v_n` at
prime level, weight 2); `scope = tag=<U_p:…>` — the bounds attached to $U_p$-eigenvalue tags of the full algebra at $N>1$.

### 3.4 Torsion — `torsion_points.tsv`

One line per (record, $q$, $\ell$, torsion point): the torsion Hecke eigensystem `system` (residues of the $T_p$), its `relation_to_free`
(`distinct_certified`, `common_certified`, `agrees_on_P` — agreement on the computed operators only, not a certificate), `generation_tors`,
`route`, `witness`; the universal-coefficient data `split` and `hom_order`; `tor_ref` (where the descent holds the Tor term); and the glue
$J$ (`J_status`, `J_lower`, `J_upper`; always a sandwich, never an exact value). `pt = -` marks a $(q,\ell)$ entry without points.

`split` says whether the extension of the free part by the torsion point splits, and **for which algebra** ([C] `sec:recordschema` item 8,
v1.25.0-pre.4; the vocabulary of `Integral/HeckeZRecord.cpp`):

| `split` | meaning |
|---|---|
| `split_by_support` | the point is `distinct_certified` from every eigensystem of the free part: the extension splits for $\mathbb T$ because the supports are disjoint |
| `split_on_P` | **final**, a statement about $\mathbb T' = \mathbb Z[T_p : p \in$ the read set $\mathcal P]$, like `agrees_on_P`: the extension splits $\mathbb T'$-equivariantly at the point. It is *not* a statement about $\mathbb T$: that needs $\mathcal P$ to generate the image of $\mathbb T$ on $H^1_\ell/\ell^K$ with the torsion included (the mixed generation, a conjecture in [C]), which the generation of G2 (of $\mathbb T^{\mathrm{fr}}$ on $L$) does not give |
| `non_split` | the extension does not split (for $\mathbb T$) |
| `split` | split for $\mathbb T$; kept for a later theorem — v1 never writes it |
| `blocked(export)`, `refused(no_lift)`, `refused(export_word)`, `not_run` | not read (`not_run` at $q = 2$, and wherever v1 does not compute it) |

Edition 1 has `split_on_P` 359, `split_by_support` 78, `non_split` 12, `not_run` 287 lines.

### 3.5 Compact support — `hc.tsv`

G7: `theory = H_c`, `hecke`, `pair`. No route computes a Hecke action on $H^2_c$ torsion; the record says `not_run`.

### 3.6 Certificates — `certs.tsv`

The G9 summary per record: `ranks`, the Lean caps, how many K1 / K2 / K3 / K6–K7 entries the record carries, which checkers are `absent`
and why, the trust handles `tb_chi` / `tb_mat`, the number of G9 fields, and `lean_joined` — the Lean status from
`lean/cells/HZ/manifest.tsv`, **joined** (not record content; it changes without changing any record hash).

### 3.7 Coherence with the mod-$\ell$ census — `join_modl.tsv`

Census 4.0 is the mod-$\ell$ Hecke census at level 1. For every level-1 record and every $\ell$ of `badset.tsv`, the multiset of
(residue degree . length) of the record's maximal ideals is compared with the census ideals of $H^1$ at $(n,\ell)$:

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| `equal` | the same multiset |
| `census_extra(torsion_q1)` | the census has additional ideals, and integral $H^1$ has $\ell$-torsion (integral census ed.1) with at least as many summands as the extra dimension: mod-$\ell$ cohomology sees $H^1\otimes\mathbb F_\ell \supsetneq L\otimes\mathbb F_\ell$ — the torsion classes carry eigensystems the free-part algebra does not |
| `census_extra(unexplained)`, `mismatch` | a defect — the builder stops |
| `not_decomposed(<status>)` | the record did not decompose at $\ell$ |
| `not_in_census40` | $(n,\ell)$ outside census 4.0 |

`census_ideal_ids` names the census rows; `extra` lists the extra ideals; `ed1_tors_q1`, `ed1_tors_q2` the integral torsion at $\ell$. This is
the coherence the long-term design asks for: the integral object and its reductions recorded so that the mod-$\ell$ modules can be matched.

### 3.8 The records, the descent inputs and the coverage — `cells.tsv`, bundles, `not_computed.tsv`

* `records_bundle.tar.gz` holds every record file byte for byte (`records/<lane>/<id>.json`); `cells.tsv` gives member, size, sha256,
  `record_fnv1a64`, `cert_sha256` and status. Every number of every table can be re-derived from it.
* A record references its integral descent cell. When that cell is in the integral census ed.1, `descent_cell_sha256` is its sha256 there
  (`descent_join = ed1`). Otherwise the descent cell was regenerated with the run's binary and is in `descent_bundle.tar.gz`
  (`descent_cells.tsv`); its `record_fnv1a64` equals the record's `descent_record_fnv1a64` (`descent_join = descent_bundle`).
* `cells.tsv` also says **which program wrote the record**: `pass` (the HZ-RUN pass that wrote the file), `binary_sha256` (full sha256
  of that `cohomology_app`) and `source_pin` (the commit it was built from); `rows.tsv` repeats `pass` and `source_pin`. They are derived from the run's own driver log
  (shipped as `provenance_driver_log.txt`): each pass opens with a `RUN driver … binary_sha256=` line, and a record was written in the pass
  whose `CELL end <lane>/<cell> status=done` line shows it computed (`ran` = the cell's record count). A record resumed in a later pass keeps
  its file and its original pass. Records carry no binary hash of their own (G10 has none).
* `not_computed.tsv` lists every planned record that the edition does not contain. Planned records = the records of the bundle + these,
  disjointly. Columns: `N`, `n`, `algebra`, `id` (the id the record would have), `kind`, `lane`, `pred_wall_s`, `reason`.
  * `kind = deferred_before_run` — taken out of the plan before the run; the rows are exactly the run kit's `defer_pass2.tsv` (shipped as
    `provenance_defer_pass2.tsv`), one per planned algebra, with its reason text; `lane` and `pred_wall_s` are `-` (never scheduled).
  * `kind = planned_not_finished` — scheduled but without a finished record; the rows are exactly the `missing` lists of the run's
    `verify.json` (shipped as `provenance_verify.json`). `lane` is the run lane it was scheduled on, `pred_wall_s` the cost model's
    predicted wall time **of that record** from the run plan (`plan/manifest.tsv`). A cell with two algebras has two records, each with
    its own share: for $(2089,0)$ they are 1 143 112.8 s and 1 145 037.9 s, together the cell's 2 288 151 s (≈ 635 h) of the `reason`.
* `big.tsv.gz`: the values longer than 4096 characters; a table cell then reads `big:<first 16 hex of sha256>`.

## 4. Files and checks

Every table `.tsv` (also inside `.gz`) starts with `# schema=integral_hecke_dataset/v1 table=<t> rows=<r> tool=… edition=…`, then the header.
`manifest.json` holds provenance (`record_binaries`: each pass's binary sha256, source pin and record count; the binary that re-accepted
every record in the run's verify step; the schema tag; the sha256 of every input), coverage, the `not_computed` section, the builder's
checks, row counts and the sha256 + size of every file; `MANIFEST.sha256` lists them. Three files are byte copies of the run's own
provenance, so the edition can be checked without the run directory: `provenance_driver_log.txt` (the run's `logs/driver.log`),
`provenance_verify.json` (`verify.json`) and `provenance_defer_pass2.tsv` (the kit's `defer_pass2.tsv`); they are not tables. Deterministic: no timestamps, sorted
rows, gzip and tar headers zeroed; building twice gives identical bytes.

* `mk_hecke_dataset.py verify DIR` — the files against the manifest (`HZDS_DIR_VERIFY … verdict=OK`).
* `hz_dataset_verify.py DIR [--run WORK] [--binary B --scratch S]` — the independent check (it imports nothing from the builder): every
  record re-hashed by its own code; each record file's name bound to its content (record id, cell key, algebra, settings fingerprint);
  **every row and column of every table re-derived from the records** and compared as whole rows, so a changed value, a missing and an
  extra (orphan) row all fail; the status rule; the 20 pinned record hashes; the coverage against the pinned cell list; the not-computed
  section against the run's own `defer_pass2.tsv` and `verify.json` (their sha256 pinned in the verifier); the `pass` column re-derived from
  the driver log; the ed.1 and descent joins; the census join recomputed. With `--run WORK` it also compares the provenance copies and every
  record file with the run directory byte for byte. With `--binary` the pass-2 binary re-accepts every record (`resume`: it re-hashes the
  file, it does **not** recompute it). One line `HZDS_VERIFY … bad=0 … verdict=OK`.
* `hz_dataset_verify.py DIR --selftest S` — negative controls on scratch copies (each tamper, with the manifest regenerated as a forger
  would, must make the check fail; the unedited copy must pass): `SELFTEST hz_dataset_verify … verdict=OK`.
